Tinos is metrically compatible with Times New Roman. It is bundled here only as a
fallback for platforms that ship neither Times New Roman nor Liberation Serif —
in practice, Android. Browsers on macOS and Windows resolve Times New Roman
locally and never request these files.

Subsets: latin, latin-ext. Weights: 400, 700. Styles: normal, italic.
Source: Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Tinos), v26.
